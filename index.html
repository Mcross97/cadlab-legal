Privacy Policy — Fusion Add-ins

Applies to the Autodesk Fusion add-ins published by CADLAB: Mesh Reverse-Engineering Toolkit, Database Archive Tool, Tubular Maker.
Last updated: August 11, 2026.

Data we collect

Our add-ins do not collect, store or transmit any personal data, design data, usage analytics or telemetry.

All processing (mesh fitting, exports, tube-frame generation) happens locally on your computer. Your designs, scans and files never leave your machine.

Subscription validation

The only network communication performed by our add-ins is a call to the Autodesk App Store Entitlement API (an Autodesk service) to verify that your App Store subscription is active. This call transmits your Autodesk user ID and the app identifier to Autodesk — no other data. Autodesk's handling of this data is governed by the Autodesk Privacy Statement.

The result of this check (valid/invalid and its date) is cached locally on your machine so the add-in can keep working offline for up to 14 days. This cache contains no personal data beyond your Autodesk user ID and never leaves your computer.

Third parties

We do not share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any. Our add-ins embed no analytics tools, no advertising networks and no third-party SDKs that communicate over the network.

Data retention and deletion

We retain no user data on our side — there is nothing to retain. The local subscription cache is deleted when you uninstall the add-in (or can be deleted manually at any time by removing the add-in's folder).

Your rights — consent and deletion

Since we collect no data, there is no consent to revoke and no server-side data to delete. Uninstalling the add-in removes everything it stored locally. For any privacy question or request, contact us at matt.cadlab@gmail.com — we answer within a few business days.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above (for example by adding optional telemetry), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be stated in the version notes.
